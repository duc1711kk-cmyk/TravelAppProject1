TRAVELAPP - BAN KHUNG MUC DO VUA

MUC DICH
- Giu nguyen giao dien Java Swing va cac diem ket noi/su kien hien co.
- Giu nguyen ten class, ten ham, tham so, kieu tra ve va constructor.
- De trong logic nghiep vu de 5 thanh vien tu hoan thien.
- FileHandler.java duoc giu nguyen de lam lop doc/ghi file co ban.
- Destination.java va cac getter/setter duoc giu nguyen.
- Itinerary.addDay() duoc giu nguyen vi day la thao tac nen de tao ngay va tranh lam hong luong khoi tao.

CAC FILE NHOM CAN HOAN THIEN

1) service/SearchService.java
   - searchByName(): tim theo ten, khong phan biet hoa/thuong.
   - filterByCategory(): loc theo danh muc; xu ly truong hop "Tat ca".

2) service/BudgetService.java
   - suggestKnapsack(): cai dat quy hoach dong Knapsack 0/1 de chon dia diem trong ngan sach.

3) service/ItineraryService.java
   - addToDay(): kiem tra chi so ngay, tranh trung dia diem, them vao DayPlan.
   - isOverBudget(): so sanh tong chi phi voi ngan sach.
   - autoDistribute(): chia danh sach dia diem vao cac ngay theo so luong moi ngay.
   - exportText(): tao chuoi van ban mo ta lich trinh va chi phi.

4) service/RatingService.java
   - rate(): kiem tra diem 1-5, cap nhat diem trung binh va so luot danh gia, luu du lieu.

5) data/DestinationManager.java
   - loadDestinationsFromFile(): doc dong du lieu, tach cac cot, tao Destination va nap ratingCounts.
   - saveDestinationsToFile(): chuyen danh sach Destination thanh cac dong va ghi file.

6) data/UserManager.java
   - toggleFavorite(): them yeu thich neu chua co, sau do luu.
   - removeFavoriteById(): xoa yeu thich theo ID, sau do luu.
   - save(): luu yeu thich va lich trinh theo dinh dang FAV / ITIN / DAY.
   - load(): doc dinh dang tren va khoi phuc User, Itinerary.

7) model/DayPlan.java
   - addDestination(): them dia diem, khong cho phep trung.
   - removeDestination(): xoa dia diem.
   - getTotalCost(): cong chi phi cac dia diem trong ngay.

8) model/Itinerary.java
   - removeDay(): xoa ngay va danh so lai.
   - getTotalCost(): cong chi phi tat ca cac ngay.
   - findDayContaining(): tim ngay dang chua dia diem, tra -1 neu khong co.

9) model/User.java
   - addFavorite(): them yeu thich, tranh trung.
   - removeFavorite(): xoa yeu thich.

PHAN DUOC GIU NGUYEN
- Toan bo src/com/travelapp/ui/
- Main.java va cac ket noi su kien trong MainFrame.java
- FileHandler.java
- Destination.java
- Constructor, getter, setter va cac cau truc du lieu can thiet khac.

LUU Y QUAN TRONG

- Cac chuc nang lien quan se chua hoat dong dung cho den khi nhom code xong.
- Khong doi ten ham/tham so/kieu tra ve neu chua thong nhat voi nguoi phu trach giao dien.
- Khi code xong tung phan, chay thu lai cac man hinh va cac tinh huong loi.
- FileHandler duoc giu lai de nhom tap trung vao nghiep vu va cach quan ly du lieu; neu muon tu code ca doc/ghi file thi can thong nhat rieng.
